    Method

    1. Mix. Warm the milk to about 30°C. Combine everything except the cornmeal in the mixer with the dough hook, low speed, 3 to 5 minutes. The dough stays sticky.
    2. Rise. Cover and keep warm (25 to 27°C) until puffy, about 2 hours.
    3. Cut. Roll to 1.5 cm on cornmeal and cut 7 to 8 cm rounds. Rest 30 minutes.
    4. Griddle. Dry skillet, low-medium heat, 6 to 8 minutes a side, until about 96°C inside.
    5. Cool, then split with a fork. Freeze split muffins in a single layer, then bag. Toast from frozen.
